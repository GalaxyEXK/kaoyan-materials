## 施密特正交化：

施密特（Schmidt）标准正交化方法

设向量组 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性无关, 其标准正交化的方法如下(又称正交规范化):

先正交化,取

$$
\boldsymbol {\beta} _ {1} = \alpha_ {1},
$$

$$
\pmb {\beta} _ {2} = \pmb {\alpha} _ {2} - \frac {(\pmb {\alpha} _ {2} , \pmb {\beta} _ {1})}{(\pmb {\beta} _ {1} , \pmb {\beta} _ {1})} \pmb {\beta} _ {1},
$$

$$
\pmb {\beta} _ {3} = \pmb {\alpha} _ {3} - \frac {(\pmb {\alpha} _ {3} , \pmb {\beta} _ {1})}{(\pmb {\beta} _ {1} , \pmb {\beta} _ {1})} \pmb {\beta} _ {1} - \frac {(\pmb {\alpha} _ {3} , \pmb {\beta} _ {2})}{(\pmb {\beta} _ {2} , \pmb {\beta} _ {2})} \pmb {\beta} _ {2},
$$

则 $\beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \beta_{3}$ 是正交向量组.

再将 $\beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \beta_{3}$ 单位化. 取

$$
\boldsymbol {\eta} _ {1} = \frac {\boldsymbol {\beta} _ {1}}{| \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {1} |}, \boldsymbol {\eta} _ {2} = \frac {\boldsymbol {\beta} _ {2}}{| \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {2} |}, \boldsymbol {\eta} _ {3} = \frac {\boldsymbol {\beta} _ {3}}{| \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {3} |},
$$

则 $\eta_{1},\eta_{2},\eta_{3}$ 是标准正交向量组，即有 $(\eta_{i},\eta_{j})=\left\{\begin{aligned}&0,&\quad i\neq j,\\ &1,&\quad i=j.\end{aligned}\right.$

## 4. 核心思想：减掉“投影部分”

假设有两个向量：

$$

$$

先取：

$$
\beta_ {1} = \alpha_ {1}
$$

然后要让第二个向量和 $\beta_{1}$ 垂直，就把 $\alpha_{2}$ 在 $\beta_{1}$ 方向上的投影减掉：

$$
\beta_ {2} = \alpha_ {2} - \frac {\left(\alpha_ {2} , \beta_ {1}\right)}{\left(\beta_ {1} , \beta_ {1}\right)} \beta_ {1}
$$

这个式子最重要。

其中：

$$
\frac {(\alpha_ {2} , \beta_ {1})}{(\beta_ {1} , \beta_ {1})} \beta_ {1}

所以：

$$
\beta_ {2} = \alpha_ {2} - \mathrm{投影}

减掉同方向部分后，剩下的部分就和 $\beta_{1}$ 垂直。
